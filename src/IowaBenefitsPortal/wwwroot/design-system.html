Design tokens

  • Ink #1a2a3a on white — contrast about 13.6:1
  • Primary #0b4f6c on white — contrast about 8.2:1
  • Accent #7a2e00 on white — contrast about 8.9:1
  • Focus: 3px gold #b7791f outline with 3px offset, always visible
  • Breakpoints: 30rem (small) and 48rem (two-column cards)
